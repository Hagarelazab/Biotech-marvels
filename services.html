Service 01

Primer Design

Structured primer design support for PCR-based molecular biology workflows, with attention to sequence selection and key primer-quality parameters.

🔬

What the service covers

Target sequence review

Review the supplied gene or target sequence and identify the relevant region for primer planning.

Primer candidate selection

Generate candidate primer pairs based on the requested experimental objective.

Basic quality parameters

Evaluate primer length, melting temperature, GC content, and expected amplicon characteristics.

Specificity-focused review

Check candidate suitability against the intended target and flag obvious design concerns.

Deliverables

What you receive

✓
Forward primer sequence
Clearly labeled 5′→3′ sequence.
✓
Reverse primer sequence
Clearly labeled 5′→3′ sequence.
✓
Primer properties
Key design parameters summarized in a clean format.
✓
Design notes
Important considerations or limitations identified during review.
Important: Final experimental performance depends on sample quality, PCR conditions, reagents, target complexity, and laboratory validation.

Before requesting

Information that helps us understand your design request.

The more clearly the experimental target is defined, the more efficiently the request can be reviewed.

Gene / target

Gene name, accession number, or target sequence.

Organism

Species or biological source of the target.

PCR objective

The intended application or experimental purpose.

Constraints

Any known target region, amplicon preference, or other requirements.
